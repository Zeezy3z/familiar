Familiar

    Choose your familiars

    Familiar can talk to several agents. Tick the ones you want to use in game — each signs in with its own account (a subscription via your browser, or an API key in that tool's own settings). Familiar never installs agents for you. Sign-ins stay in each agent’s own files; prompts go to the agent you choose.

    Quiet play
    Replies wait in your catch-up notes when you open /fam. Approvals and failures keep their attention cues; agents continue working.
    Private mode
    Zones show as Project 1, 2, ... and quests as Quest #1, #2, ...; desktop threads are hidden, notices are generic and nothing is spoken. Approval cards still show the full request. Also /fam private on | off.
    New quests start in
    Auto lets Claude's safety check approve routine actions and still asks you about risky ones. Change any quest with Mode.
    Approve key
    Approve key (as bound in WoW, e.g. F9 or CTRL-F9): type the key you bound to Approve pending request (Options → Keybindings → AddOns → Familiar). Familiar checks it was really pressed. Without it, approve by clicking the card or the Approve button.
    Sounds
    Notices
    Also in game: /fam font bigger | smaller | reset, /fam sound on | off, /fam warnings on | off

    Find your way back

    /fam opens your Quest Log; the minimap imp does too. Pick a quest to read or continue it. Text opens a copyable transcript from the last /reload. Ctrl+Up recalls your last message.
    Close this panel to keep Familiar in the tray. Double-click Run Familiar.cmd or choose Open Familiar from the tray to return.

    Companion controls

    Companion source updates take effect after a full Familiar restart. Once quests have settled, choose Quit from the tray, then open Run Familiar.cmd. Opening the launcher while Familiar runs only opens this panel. Reload config changes settings; it does not reload app code. Addon changes load on your next WoW launch or a user-initiated /reload.
